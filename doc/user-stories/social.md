# User Stories: Social & Sharing

## US-018: Share Meal Plans

**As a** user  
**I want to** share my meal plans with others  
**So that** I can help friends or get feedback

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Export meal plan as shareable link
- Share recipes with community
- Copy meal plans from other users
- Rate and review shared plans

---

## US-019: Community Challenges

**As a** user  
**I want to** participate in nutrition challenges  
**So that** I can stay motivated

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Join community challenges
- Track progress with others
- Leaderboards and achievements
- Share progress updates
