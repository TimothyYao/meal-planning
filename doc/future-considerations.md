# Future Considerations

This document outlines additional ideas, opportunities, and considerations for the Macro Planning App beyond the core roadmap.

## Machine Learning & AI

### Personalized Recommendations
- **Meal Suggestions**: ML models trained on user preferences, goals, and past behavior
- **Macro Optimization**: AI-powered suggestions to balance macros throughout the day
- **Recipe Generation**: Generate custom recipes based on available ingredients and preferences
- **Predictive Analytics**: Predict which meal plans will help users achieve their goals

### Natural Language Processing
- **Voice Input**: Parse spoken meal descriptions ("I had a chicken breast with rice")
- **Recipe Parsing**: Extract ingredients and macros from recipe text or URLs
- **Smart Search**: Understand user intent in food searches ("high protein breakfast")

### Computer Vision
- **Food Recognition**: Identify foods from photos and estimate portions
- **Barcode Scanning**: Enhanced barcode scanning with image recognition fallback
- **Nutrition Label Reading**: OCR to extract nutrition facts from labels

## Integration Ecosystem

### Smart Kitchen Appliances
- **Smart Scales**: Connect to kitchen scales for accurate portion measurement
- **Smart Ovens**: Sync cooking times and temperatures with meal plans
- **Meal Prep Devices**: Integration with meal prep appliances (e.g., meal prep containers with QR codes)

### Voice Assistants
- **Alexa Skills**: "Alexa, log my breakfast" or "What are my macros today?"
- **Google Assistant**: Voice commands for meal logging and queries
- **Siri Shortcuts**: Quick actions for iOS users

### Wearable Devices
- **Apple Watch**: 
  - Quick meal logging from watch
  - Macro progress complications
  - Activity-based calorie adjustments
- **Fitbit**: 
  - Sync activity data
  - Adjust macros based on calories burned
- **Garmin**: Integration with fitness tracking

### Health Apps
- **Apple Health**: Bidirectional sync with HealthKit
- **Google Fit**: Integration with Android health data
- **MyFitnessPal**: Import/export data compatibility
- **Strava**: Sync with fitness activities

## Meal Delivery & Services

### Meal Delivery Partnerships
- **Meal Kit Services**: Integration with HelloFresh, Blue Apron, etc.
- **Prepared Meal Services**: Connect with Freshly, Factor, etc.
- **Grocery Delivery**: Enhanced integration with Instacart, Amazon Fresh, etc.
- **Restaurant Delivery**: Integration with DoorDash, Uber Eats for macro tracking

### Restaurant Integration
- **Menu Database**: Comprehensive database of restaurant menu items with macros
- **Chain Restaurant APIs**: Direct integration with major chains
- **QR Code Menus**: Scan QR codes at restaurants to view macros
- **Location-Based Suggestions**: Suggest restaurants based on location and macro needs
